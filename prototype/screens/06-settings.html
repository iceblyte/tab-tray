Tab Tray

v1.0.0 · MIT · 分组侧边栏 + 紧凑固定行Group sidebar + compact pinned row
常规General
语言Language
界面文案语言；「自动」跟随 Obsidian 语言设置。UI language; "Auto" follows the Obsidian language setting.
启用紧凑固定行Compact pinned tabs
将已固定的标签在标签栏左侧收缩为图标，悬停显示完整文件名；右键/中键保持原生行为。关闭后恢复 Obsidian 原生样式。Shrink pinned tabs into icons on the left of the tab bar, with full-name tooltips; right/middle click stay native. Turning off restores the native style.
启用紧凑固定行（降级态示意）Compact pinned tabs (degraded sample)
特性检测失败时：开关自动关闭并置灰 + 可见提示；分组功能不受任何影响，升级 Obsidian 后自动恢复。On detection failure: toggled off and greyed with a visible notice; grouping unaffected, restored after upgrading Obsidian.
⚠ 紧凑固定行在当前 Obsidian 版本不可用，不影响分组功能。 ⚠ Compact pinned tabs are unavailable in this Obsidian version — grouping is unaffected.
成员数软阈值Soft member limit
单个分组的成员数超过该值时提示一次，但不阻止添加。Warn once when a group exceeds this member count, but never block.
数据与持久化Data & persistence
分组数据保存于 .obsidian/plugins/tab-tray/data.json：每次变更 ≤500ms 内防抖原子写盘，并轮换 data.json.bak 备份；主文件损坏时自动回退备份并提示；检测到同步冲突副本时加载最新可解析版本并提示。卸载或禁用插件不会删除数据文件。 Group data lives in .obsidian/plugins/tab-tray/data.json: every change is debounced and atomically written within 500ms, rotating a data.json.bak copy; corrupted mains fall back to the backup with a notice; sync-conflict copies are arbitrated by loading the newest parsable version. Uninstalling or disabling the plugin never deletes your data.
