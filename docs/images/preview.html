Why tabs pile up

Research, writing and a project move at the same time. Twenty tabs later the tab bar is a queue, not a map, and finding the one you want costs more than reopening it.

Groups fix the shape of the problem: a handful of named buckets you open and close in one gesture. The hard part is not the gesture — it is that the buckets are still there tomorrow.

[!info] The three tabs of “Research” are wrapped in a blue capsule — that is the group colour, painted on the host tab header.
